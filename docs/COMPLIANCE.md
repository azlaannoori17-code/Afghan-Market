# QAVENTRIX — Global Legal and Regulatory Compliance Architecture

## 1. Overview

QAVENTRIX will operate as a global multi-vendor marketplace and must provide a flexible compliance framework that supports applicable laws and regulations across different countries and jurisdictions.

The compliance system must help the platform, sellers, and business partners understand and follow their legal responsibilities.

## 2. Compliance Objectives

The system should support:

- Global regulatory compliance
- Country-specific legal requirements
- Consumer protection
- Seller compliance
- Data protection
- Product safety
- Financial compliance
- Fraud prevention
- Regulatory reporting
- Legal risk management

## 3. Country and Jurisdiction Management

The platform should maintain configurable compliance requirements for:

- Countries
- States and provinces
- Cities where applicable
- Economic regions
- Special regulatory jurisdictions

Each jurisdiction may have different requirements for marketplace operations, sellers, products, payments, and customer protection.

## 4. Business and Seller Compliance

Sellers may be required to provide:

- Identity verification
- Business registration documents
- Commercial licenses
- Tax registration information
- Business address
- Contact information
- Beneficial ownership information where legally required

Verification requirements must depend on the seller's country, business type, and applicable laws.

## 5. Product Compliance

The platform should support product compliance checks for:

- Restricted products
- Prohibited products
- Product safety requirements
- Certification requirements
- Product labeling
- Age-restricted products
- Import and export restrictions
- Intellectual property rights
- Country-specific product regulations

Products that violate applicable policies or laws must be restricted or removed.

## 6. Consumer Protection

The platform should support applicable consumer protection requirements, including:

- Transparent product information
- Accurate pricing
- Clear shipping information
- Return and refund policies
- Complaint handling
- Customer rights
- Seller accountability
- Transparent marketplace terms

## 7. Data Protection and Privacy

The compliance architecture should support applicable privacy and data protection regulations.

Key requirements:

- Transparent privacy policies
- Appropriate consent management
- Personal data access controls
- Data retention policies
- Secure data processing
- Data deletion workflows where legally required
- Data breach response procedures
- Cross-border data transfer controls

## 8. Financial and Payment Compliance

The platform should support applicable financial regulations relating to:

- Payment processing
- Seller payouts
- Refunds
- Financial record keeping
- Fraud prevention
- Transaction monitoring
- Tax reporting
- Payment provider requirements

Regulated financial activities must only be performed where legally authorized.

## 9. Fraud Prevention and Risk Management

The system should help identify:

- Suspicious seller accounts
- Fake business information
- Fraudulent transactions
- Account takeover attempts
- Payment abuse
- Identity verification anomalies
- Suspicious refund patterns
- Marketplace manipulation

High-risk cases should be reviewed by authorized personnel.

## 10. Intellectual Property Compliance

The platform should provide processes for handling:

- Trademark complaints
- Copyright complaints
- Counterfeit product reports
- Unauthorized product listings
- Intellectual property disputes
- Repeat violations

Sellers must be able to respond to legitimate complaints through appropriate review procedures.

## 11. Regulatory Monitoring

The compliance system should support:

- Regulatory requirement updates
- Country-specific policy changes
- Compliance deadlines
- Legal review records
- Internal compliance alerts
- Regulatory change history

Qualified legal professionals should review important regulatory changes before implementation.

## 12. Compliance Policies

The platform should maintain documented policies for:

- Seller onboarding
- Product listings
- Prohibited products
- Consumer protection
- Privacy
- Data security
- Payments
- Refunds
- Advertising
- Intellectual property
- Account suspension

Policies should be versioned and available in supported languages.

## 13. Compliance Review and Enforcement

Authorized compliance personnel should be able to:

- Review seller documentation
- Investigate reported violations
- Request additional information
- Restrict product listings
- Suspend non-compliant accounts
- Record enforcement decisions
- Review appeals
- Escalate serious cases

Enforcement decisions must be documented and auditable.

## 14. Compliance Notifications

The platform may notify relevant users about:

- Missing verification documents
- Expiring business licenses
- Policy updates
- Compliance review requests
- Product restrictions
- Account restrictions
- Regulatory deadlines
- Required corrective actions

Notifications should integrate with the global notification system.

## 15. Database Architecture

Suggested database entities:

- ComplianceJurisdictions
- ComplianceRequirements
- CompliancePolicies
- CompliancePolicyVersions
- ComplianceAssessments
- ComplianceChecks
- ComplianceDocuments
- ComplianceCases
- ComplianceViolations
- ComplianceActions
- ComplianceAppeals
- RegulatoryUpdates
- ComplianceDeadlines
- ComplianceAuditLogs

Records should be linked to relevant users, sellers, stores, products, orders, and jurisdictions.

## 16. Security and Auditability

The compliance system must include:

- Role-based access control
- Secure document storage
- Encryption of sensitive information
- Detailed audit logs
- Restricted access to confidential records
- Document retention controls
- Secure administrative workflows

Compliance records must be protected against unauthorized modification.

## 17. Reporting and Analytics

The platform should measure:

- Seller verification status
- Compliance review volume
- Product violations
- Open compliance cases
- Resolution times
- Policy enforcement actions
- Regulatory deadlines
- Regional compliance risks

Reports should support authorized internal review and legally required reporting.

## 18. Integrations

The compliance system should integrate with:

- User management
- Seller management
- Products
- Payments
- Taxes
- Orders
- Returns and refunds
- Shipping
- Support
- Security
- Notifications
- Analytics

## 19. Automation and AI

Future capabilities may include:

- Automated document classification
- Product compliance risk detection
- Regulatory change monitoring
- Suspicious activity detection
- Compliance workflow automation
- Policy recommendation systems

AI-generated assessments must be reviewed by qualified personnel when legal rights or obligations are affected.

## 20. Reliability and Scalability

The architecture should support:

- Multiple regulatory jurisdictions
- Versioned compliance rules
- Reliable document processing
- Background verification workflows
- Audit history preservation
- Monitoring and error tracking
- Scalable compliance services

## 21. Future Development

Planned improvements:

- Country-specific compliance integrations
- Automated regulatory monitoring
- Advanced risk dashboards
- International verification providers
- Improved compliance automation
- Dedicated compliance management portal

## 22. Core Principle

QAVENTRIX must operate responsibly, transparently, and securely while respecting applicable laws and regulations in every market where it conducts business.
