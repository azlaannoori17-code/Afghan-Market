# Afghan Market – Global Product Management Architecture

## 1. Product Management Strategy
- Support products from sellers worldwide.
- Support the official Afghan Market store.
- Provide centralized product management.
- Support multiple languages and currencies.
- Maintain accurate product information.
- Support domestic and international marketplaces.

## 2. Product Ownership
Every product must belong to:
- An independent seller.
- The official Afghan Market store.

The system must identify product ownership and enforce access permissions.

## 3. Product Information
Each product should support:
- Product name.
- Short description.
- Full description.
- Unique product identifier.
- SKU.
- Barcode.
- Brand.
- Category.
- Product images.
- Product videos.
- Product specifications.
- Country of origin.
- Product condition.
- Product status.

## 4. Categories and Subcategories
- Support multiple product categories.
- Support unlimited category nesting.
- Support parent-child category relationships.
- Support multilingual category names.
- Allow administrators to manage categories.
- Support category images and descriptions.
- Prevent invalid category relationships.

## 5. Brands and Manufacturers
- Support international brands.
- Support manufacturer information.
- Support brand logos.
- Support multilingual brand names.
- Allow administrators to manage brands.
- Support brand verification where necessary.

## 6. Product Variants
Products should support:
- Different sizes.
- Different colors.
- Different materials.
- Different models.
- Different configurations.
- Different packaging options.

Each variant must support its own SKU, price, and inventory.

## 7. Product Pricing
- Support multiple currencies.
- Use ISO 4217 currency codes.
- Support regular prices.
- Support discounted prices.
- Support scheduled discounts.
- Support seller-specific pricing.
- Maintain original price information.
- Keep currency conversion separate from product records.

## 8. Inventory Management
- Track available stock.
- Track reserved stock.
- Track sold quantities.
- Support multiple warehouses.
- Support stock adjustments.
- Record inventory movements.
- Prevent sales beyond available stock.
- Notify sellers about low inventory.

## 9. Product Media
- Support multiple product images.
- Support product videos.
- Support image galleries.
- Support image ordering.
- Support image optimization.
- Support different image sizes.
- Use secure file storage.
- Validate uploaded file types and sizes.

## 10. Product Specifications
- Support structured product attributes.
- Support category-specific specifications.
- Support technical specifications.
- Support measurement units.
- Support multilingual specifications.
- Allow administrators to configure product attributes.

## 11. Product Search and Filtering
- Search by product name.
- Search by SKU and barcode.
- Filter by category.
- Filter by brand.
- Filter by price.
- Filter by seller.
- Filter by country.
- Filter by product condition.
- Filter by availability.
- Support sorting and pagination.
- Support multilingual search.

## 12. Product Reviews and Ratings
- Allow eligible customers to review products.
- Support star ratings.
- Support written reviews.
- Support review moderation.
- Prevent duplicate reviews where applicable.
- Allow customers to report inappropriate reviews.
- Display verified purchase indicators.

## 13. Product Approval and Moderation
Support the following product states:
- Draft.
- Pending review.
- Approved.
- Rejected.
- Published.
- Suspended.
- Archived.

Administrators must be able to:
- Review submitted products.
- Approve or reject products.
- Request corrections.
- Suspend inappropriate listings.
- Record moderation decisions.

## 14. International Product Support
- Support products from different countries.
- Support multiple languages.
- Support country of origin.
- Support international product identification.
- Support different measurement systems.
- Support country-specific product requirements.
- Support import and export information where applicable.

## 15. Product Shipping Information
Each product should support:
- Weight.
- Length.
- Width.
- Height.
- Package dimensions.
- Shipping restrictions.
- Country of origin.
- Handling requirements.

Product shipping information must integrate with the global shipping system.

## 16. Seller Product Management
Sellers must be able to:
- Create products.
- Edit their own products.
- Upload product media.
- Manage product variants.
- Update prices.
- Manage inventory.
- View product performance.
- Manage product availability.

Sellers must not modify products belonging to other sellers.

## 17. Official Store Management
- Support products owned by Afghan Market.
- Allow authorized administrators to manage official products.
- Maintain separate official store inventory.
- Support independent pricing and promotions.
- Clearly identify official store products.

## 18. Product Data Quality and Security
- Validate product information.
- Prevent duplicate product identifiers where applicable.
- Validate uploaded files.
- Enforce seller ownership.
- Protect product management APIs.
- Maintain product change history.
- Record important administrative actions.

## 19. Marketplace Integration
Product management must integrate with:
- Seller accounts.
- Customer accounts.
- Categories and brands.
- Shopping cart.
- Orders.
- Inventory.
- Payments.
- Shipping.
- Search.
- Reviews.
- Promotions.
- Analytics.

## 20. Future Capabilities
- AI-powered product recommendations.
- Visual product search.
- Barcode scanning.
- Advanced product comparison.
- Bulk product imports.
- Automated product translation.
- Product analytics.
- Supplier integrations.
- Advanced inventory forecasting.
